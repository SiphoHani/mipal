# 2-Minute Investor Pitch (about 250 words)

**Hook (10s)**
Imagine your ten-year-old on Facebook or WhatsApp. Any adult can message them, and nobody checks who is on the other side.

**Problem (20s)**
Children today get smartphones early. Mainstream platforms rely on self-declared ages, so a ten-year-old can join in seconds, and predators know it. Kids are easily manipulated with sweets, airtime or attention. Parents are left guessing.

**Solution and target user (20s)**
Meet MiPal: a social app built only for children aged 9 to 16, bought and trusted by their parents. It looks and feels like the apps kids love, with a feed, chats and games, but every account is age-checked and parent-approved.

**Key features (35s)**
Our Safety Shield blocks adult content, personal details and grooming patterns like "don't tell your parents" and alerts the parent instantly. The Safe Map shows parents where the child is and warns the child as they approach a danger zone. Pal, our friendly AI owl, helps with homework and feelings. Mini games teach kids to spot danger. And one SOS button reaches parents plus SAPS, Childline and the GBV Command Centre.

**Value (20s)**
Kids get fun and a safe community. Parents get peace of mind. We earn through a low-cost family subscription, school and NGO licences, and brand-safe partnerships. No ads aimed at kids.

**Why better (10s)**
Social apps were built for adults; parental-control apps only watch. MiPal gives children a safe place of their own.

**Close (5s)**
MiPal: chat, play, learn, stay safe. Let us make childhood online safer together.
