# Design, Safety and Privacy Notes

## Emotionally intelligent design
- **Colour:** purple (trust, imagination), sunny yellow (joy), mint (calm and success), coral (alerts, used sparingly), sky blue (curiosity).
- **Friendly tone:** rounded shapes, big tap targets, mascot (Pal the Owl), encouraging language, no shaming when content is blocked ("Try rewording your post").
- **Emotional check-in:** mood buttons on Home; sad or scared moods point to Pal and Childline.
- **Rewards:** XP, levels, badges and confetti for positive behaviour.
- **Transparency:** the child is always told that location is shared and why.

## Safety layers
1. Age gate 9-16, ID check and parent-approval code.
2. Friends by code only; no public strangers.
3. Safety Shield on posts, chat, search and Pal: adult words, phone/email/address, links, grooming phrases (secrets, gifts for photos, meet-ups).
4. Incoming unsafe messages are hidden, the sender is paused and the parent is alerted.
5. Geofenced risk zones with approach and entry alerts.
6. SOS, emergency contacts, Pal's help responses.
7. PIN-protected parent dashboard.

## Privacy principles (POPIA)
Collect the minimum, nickname instead of full name, parental consent, no ads to children, encryption and retention limits in production.

## Roadmap
1. Pilot with 2-3 Johannesburg schools.
2. Real ID/age verification and parent app.
3. ML moderation and human review team.
4. Android/iOS store release via Capacitor; add isiZulu, Sesotho, Afrikaans.
